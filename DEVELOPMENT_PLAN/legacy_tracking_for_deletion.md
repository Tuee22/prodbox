[Resume Here](README.md#resume-here) owns execution. States are `Pending`, `Ready`, `Removed`. All rows below start Pending until their actual condition is checked. The remover is the phase that deletes/retires the item; it cannot close with unresolved owned rows. A row's prerequisite is its earlier phases plus its own named replacement acceptance, never a later phase. Historical original-install-only code may survive ordinary-route replacement solely under L18/Phase 10.

Each family/individual row requires (1) replacement acceptance and applicable mutation evidence, (2) independent resource/credential disposition if any, (3) source/route/generated-reference absence, and (4) preservation of still-needed historical inputs. A missing file or old Done label is insufficient. Record exact symbols and evidence when implementing; do not delete a live retained root as a ledger operation.
